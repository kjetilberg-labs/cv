§ 01 PREFACE · BIOGRAPHICAL BRIEF

A Brief Preface

Kjetil Berg is a Norwegian software engineer, raised in Jølster on the western coast and drawn early to the particular satisfaction of making a machine do exactly what it is told. A Mensa member by credential and an unrepentant autodidact by disposition — curious rather than credentialed, and more interested in the problem on the table than the title at the top of the page.

The day-to-day is software engineering in the broad sense — backend services, fullstack web work, databases, distributed systems — but two longer threads run underneath. The first is Verse, a solo game engine in C++ and OpenGL kept alive across four summers of quiet, deliberate work. The second is The Bergen pCT Project, the subject of a recent master's thesis: a simulation toolkit for a prototype calorimeter destined for proton-CT cancer imaging.

Leads projects when the group asks for it; builds alone when it doesn't. Finds home in teams that take correctness seriously and in problems that reward patience. Presently open to engagement.

— Margin note "What I cannot create, I do not understand." — Feynman. A useful standard for anyone who prefers building to reading about building.

§ 03 SERVICE RECORD · PROFESSIONAL HISTORY

A Chronological Service Record

  1. 2024 — 2025 Entry I

    MSc Researcher · The Bergen pCT Project

    University of Bergen · Bergen

    Developed a simulation toolkit for a prototype calorimeter destined for proton-CT cancer imaging — the kind of instrument that may one day let oncologists see inside a tumour with the same particles used to treat it. A year spent at the intersection of medical physics, high-performance simulation, and stubborn numerical detail. Work documented at kjetilberg.github.io/bergen-pct.

    • C++
    • Python
    • Geant4 / ROOT
    • LaTeX
  2. 2019 — 2021 Entry II

    IoT Software Developer (Contract)

    Intin AS · Førde

    Contracted mid-degree, alongside a fellow student, to build the device API for Invite — the interface through which Intin's IoT hardware receives instructions for automated management. The work ran parallel to our studies, not instead of them. A short engagement that stretched past its deadline; we stayed on standby through deployment. He finished his bachelor's in 2023 and joined Intin properly; I went on to a master's. The platform now serves international customers.

    • Go
    • Node.js
    • TypeScript
